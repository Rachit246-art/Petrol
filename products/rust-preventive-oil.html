Rust Preventive Oils (RPO Series)

Brand / Series: KEMBOT DW 901(WET), DW 902(DRY), 904, 630, 6304, 274

Fast-acting dewatering and dry/wet protective barrier oils for inter-operational and export storage.

Industry Equivalent Standard (HPCL / IOC / BPCL / Castrol):

EQUIVALENT TO OTHER REPUTED BRANDS (Rustlick, Castrol Rustilo)

Compare Equivalent Chart →

Product Overview

Kembot RPO Series rust preventives are blended with active film-forming additives, moisture-displacing surfactants, and corrosion inhibitors dissolved in volatile hydrocarbon carriers.

Technical Specifications

Grades Available DW 901 (Wet), DW 902 (Dry), 904, 630, 6304, 274
Film Type Ultra-thin dry film / Soft oily film / Waxy
Protection Duration 6 to 24 months (Grade Dependent)
Salt Spray Test (ASTM B117) > 48 to 120 hours
Flash Point > 40 °C (Solvent based)

Performance Features & Benefits

  • ✓ Instant dewatering action displacing water drops from metal pores
  • ✓ Choice of Dry Film (DW 902) or Oily Protective Film (DW 901 / 904)
  • ✓ Exceptional salt spray and humidity cabinet rust protection (up to 12 - 24 months)
  • ✓ Easily removable with mild solvents or standard industrial wash cleaners
  • ✓ Fingerprint neutralization capability on freshly handled precision tools

Applications & Recommended Usage

  • Protection of machined metal components, bearing rings, camshafts, fasteners, and sheet metal.
  • Export shipping protection and indoor/outdoor warehouse storage.
  • Dewatering of components after aqueous machining, grinding, or washing.
Usage Guidelines: Apply by dipping, spraying, or brushing onto clean metal surfaces at ambient temperature. Allow solvent to flash off for 10–15 minutes.
