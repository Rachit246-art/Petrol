Product Overview

Kembot Grease range includes Lithium All-Purpose (AP3), Extreme Pressure (EP2, EP0, EP00), High Temperature Lithium Complex, and Copper Anti-Seize compounds for severe friction and load conditions.

Technical Specifications

Soap Thickener Type Lithium Hydroxystearate / Lithium Complex / Copper
NLGI Grades NLGI 00, 0, 1, 2, 3
Drop Point > 190 °C (Lithium) / > 260 °C (Complex)
Base Oil Viscosity @ 40°C 150 – 220 cSt
Timken OK Load 40 – 60 lbs

Performance Features & Benefits

  • ✓ High mechanical stability resisting thinning and leakage under vibration
  • ✓ Superb water-washout resistance and corrosion protection
  • ✓ High drop point (> 190 °C for AP3/EP2, > 260 °C for Lithium Complex)
  • ✓ Extreme pressure load-carrying capacity with 4-Ball weld load > 250 kgf
  • ✓ Extended re-lubrication intervals reducing downtime

Applications & Recommended Usage

  • Automotive wheel bearings, chassis lubrication, universal joints, and kingpins.
  • Industrial ball and roller bearings, centralized grease systems (EP 0, EP 00).
  • High temperature threaded connections, boiler studs, and kiln bearings (Copper Grease).
Usage Guidelines: Apply with grease gun, mechanical pump, or centralized automatic greasing unit. Do not mix incompatible soap bases.
