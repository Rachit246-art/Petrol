Industrial Gear Oils & Gear EP Series

Brand / Series: KEMBOT GEAR / GEAR EP SERIES 90, 140, 220, 320, 460

Extreme pressure industrial gear lubricants engineered for heavy shock loads.

Industry Equivalent Standard (HPCL / IOC / BPCL / Castrol):

SERVO GEAR SUPER / SPIROL EP SERIES

Compare Equivalent Chart →

Product Overview

Kembot Gear EP Series oils are formulated from solvent-extracted high VI base oils combined with sulfur-phosphorus extreme pressure additives that impart exceptional load carrying and anti-scuffing capabilities.

Technical Specifications

Performance Standard IS 8489-1989 / DIN 51517 Part 3 (CLP) / AGMA 9005-E02
Available Grades VG 90, 140, 220, 320, 460
Timken OK Load Min. 60 lbs (27 kg)
FZG Gear Test (A/8.3/90) Failure Stage > 12
4-Ball Weld Load > 250 kgf

Performance Features & Benefits

  • ✓ Outstanding Extreme-Pressure (EP) protection against micropitting and tooth scuffing
  • ✓ Excellent thermal and oxidation stability preventing sludge formation
  • ✓ Non-corrosive to yellow metals (bronze, brass, copper)
  • ✓ High demulsibility ensuring rapid separation from free water
  • ✓ Superior anti-foam and anti-rust protection

Applications & Recommended Usage

  • Enclosed spur, helical, bevel, spiral, and planetary industrial gearboxes.
  • Heavy load applications in steel mills, cement plants, mining crushers, paper machines, and sugar mills.
  • Plain and antifriction roller bearings subjected to severe shock loads (operating temp up to 90 °C).
Usage Guidelines: Select viscosity grade based on gearbox reduction ratio, operating speed, and temperature. Maintain sump levels per sight glass indicator.
