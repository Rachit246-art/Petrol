About Our Company

Leading Provider of
Industrial Solutions

Kembot Petroleum is a leading manufacturer and supplier of top-quality lubricants and specialty products for diverse sectors - Automotive, Agriculture, Aviation, Power generation, Construction, Metal working & Marine.

Our strong in-house R&D provides solutions with accurate formulations to any challenges from modern day industrial requirements or complex applications. Our advanced state-of-art machinery guarantees massive volumes with equal efficiency.

At Kembot Petroleum, we believe that energy is more than a resource — it's the driving force behind progress. As a trusted division of Kembot Global Group, we deliver petroleum products and services that combine reliability, safety, and innovation.
